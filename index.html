Palash Sharma is an AI filmmaker and editor based in Noida, India, and co-founder of Genframe.

His work moves between narrative films, brand stories and visual experiments. He combines creative direction, AI-generated imagery and editing to turn an idea into a finished film.

From the horror of One of Us to the worlds of Honeybun and Akiro's Loop, the focus stays on atmosphere, pacing and storytelling.
